## How tools get listed

- **Live product only.** No waitlists, no "coming soon."
- **Public pricing or a real free tier.** If a vendor is enterprise-sales-only, we still list it, tagged `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`, so you know what you're getting into before you book a demo.
- **Actually used for legal work**, not a general-purpose tool with one relevant use case bolted on.
- **Reviewed quarterly.** Dead links and abandoned products are removed — see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **No pay-to-list.** Sponsored placements are labeled `Sponsored` and never displace a better-fit free entry — see [Sponsors](#sponsors).

## AI Tools for Contract Review & Analysis

*AI contract-review tools read agreements, flag risky clauses, and suggest redlines so lawyers spend less time on first-pass review.*

- **[Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/)** — AI platform for law firms and in-house teams to draft, review, and analyze contracts and other legal work. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Kira Systems](https://www.kirasystems.com/)** — Litera-owned contract analysis tool that extracts clauses and data points for M&A and due-diligence review. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Luminance](https://www.luminance.com/)** — AI contract platform for drafting, negotiating, and reviewing agreements across legal, procurement, and finance teams. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Robin AI](https://robinai.com/)** — AI platform that reviews, redlines, and tracks obligations in contracts for enterprise legal teams. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Spellbook](https://spellbook.com/)** — AI add-in for Microsoft Word that drafts and reviews contract clauses inside a lawyer's existing workflow. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Legal Research & Case Law Analysis

*AI legal research tools search case law, statutes, and secondary sources and return cited answers instead of a list of documents to read.*

- **[Alexi](https://www.alexi.com/)** — AI platform for legal research, memo drafting, and document analysis built for law firms. `Paid`
- **[Bloomberg Law](https://pro.bloomberglaw.com/)** — Legal research platform with a built-in AI Assistant for case law search, drafting, and analysis. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[CoCounsel](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/products/cocounsel)** — Thomson Reuters' AI legal assistant (formerly Casetext) for research, document review, and drafting on Westlaw content. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Lexis+ with Protégé](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/lexis-plus-protege.page)** — LexisNexis legal research platform with the Protégé AI assistant for research, drafting, and analysis. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Trellis](https://trellis.law/)** — AI platform for state trial court research, judge analytics, and motion drafting for litigators. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[vLex](https://vlex.com/)** — Global legal research platform, owned by Clio, with the Vincent AI assistant for case law and analysis. `Paid`

## AI Tools for E-Discovery & Litigation Document Review

*AI e-discovery tools process, cluster, and review large litigation document sets to cut down manual first-pass review.*

- **[Casepoint](https://www.casepoint.com/)** — Cloud e-discovery platform with built-in AI analytics for corporations, government agencies, and law firms. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Clearbrief](https://clearbrief.com/)** — Word add-in that checks whether brief citations are supported by the underlying record. `Paid`
- **[DISCO](https://csdisco.com/)** — AI-powered e-discovery platform for document review, processing, and litigation workflows. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Everlaw](https://www.everlaw.com/)** — Cloud litigation platform with AI review, writing, and deposition-analysis assistants for discovery teams. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Relativity](https://www.relativity.com/)** — RelativityOne e-discovery platform with aiR generative-AI review tools for large-scale document review. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Reveal](https://www.revealdata.com/)** — E-discovery platform (owner of Logikcull) combining AI review, analytics, and self-service discovery tools. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`

## AI Tools for Legal Billing & Timekeeping Automation

*AI billing tools capture billable time automatically and turn it into client-ready invoices for law firms.*

- **[Bilr](https://www.getbilr.com/)** — Legal time-and-billing software with voice time capture and AI-assisted billing-code prediction. `Paid`
- **[Clio](https://www.clio.com/)** — Legal practice management platform with billing, timekeeping, and an AI assistant for law firms. `Paid`
- **[MyCase](https://www.mycase.com/)** — Legal practice management software whose MyCase IQ AI drafts correspondence and summarizes case documents. `Paid`
- **[Smokeball](https://www.smokeball.com/)** — Legal practice management platform with automatic time tracking and AI-assisted document drafting. `Paid`
- **[TimeSolv](https://www.timesolv.com/)** — Time, billing, and trust-accounting software built for law firms. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Legal Document Drafting & Automation

*AI drafting tools generate contracts, pleadings, and other legal documents from templates and structured client data.*

- **[Clio Draft](https://www.clio.com/draft/)** — Document automation tool (formerly Lawyaw) that fills court forms and legal documents from client data. `Paid`
- **[Gavel](https://www.gavel.io/)** — No-code document automation platform (formerly Documate) for building legal intake and drafting workflows. `Paid`
- **[Genie AI](https://www.genieai.co/)** — AI legal document drafting and review platform with a library of contract templates. `Freemium`
- **[Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com/)** — Contract lifecycle management platform with AI drafting, negotiation, and workflow tools. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Leah](https://leahai.com/)** — Agentic AI platform (formerly ContractPodAi) for contract lifecycle management across legal, procurement, and finance. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`

## AI Tools for Compliance & Regulatory Monitoring

- **[Compliance.ai](https://www.compliance.ai/)** — Archer-owned regulatory change management platform that monitors thousands of regulatory sources with AI. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Corlytics](https://www.corlytics.com/)** — Regulatory intelligence platform that aggregates global regulatory content for compliance risk analysis. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[OneTrust](https://www.onetrust.com/)** — Privacy, GRC, and AI governance platform used by legal and compliance teams to manage regulatory obligations. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[VinciWorks](https://vinciworks.com/)** — Compliance training platform with AI-driven conversational courses on regulations like GDPR and anti-bribery law. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`

## AI Tools for IP & Patent Research and Drafting

*AI patent tools search prior art and draft or analyze patent applications for IP attorneys and inventors.*

- **[IPRally](https://www.iprally.com/)** — AI-native patent search engine using graph technology to find prior art by technical concept. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[PatentPal](https://www.patentpal.com/)** — AI tool that auto-generates patent application sections such as claims and detailed descriptions. `Paid`
- **[Patlytics](https://www.patlytics.ai/)** — AI patent platform covering drafting, prosecution, infringement detection, and litigation workflows. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Rowan Patents](https://clarivate.com/intellectual-property/ip-management-software/rowan-patents/)** — Clarivate-owned AI patent drafting tool for patent practitioners. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Solve Intelligence](https://www.solveintelligence.com/)** — AI patent drafting and prosecution editor built as a Word-compatible browser tool for IP firms. `Paid`

*AI intake tools qualify leads, gather case details, and automate the first conversation between a prospective client and a law firm.*

- **[Intaker](https://www.intaker.com/)** — AI-enabled live chat and chatbot platform for law firm client intake and lead capture. `Paid`
- **[Josef](https://joseflegal.com/)** — No-code platform for building legal chatbots and self-service tools for in-house legal teams. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[LawDroid](https://lawdroid.com/)** — AI chatbot builder and copilot for client intake, consultations, and routine client communications. `Paid`
- **[Lawmatics](https://www.lawmatics.com/)** — Legal CRM with intake forms, automated workflows, and AI-assisted email drafting for law firms. `Paid`
- **[Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/)** — Virtual receptionist and intake service with AI-assisted call handling and CRM data entry for law firms. `Paid`
